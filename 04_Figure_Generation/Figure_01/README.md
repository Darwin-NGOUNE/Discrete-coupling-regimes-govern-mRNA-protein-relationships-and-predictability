## 1. Scientific Overview & Biological Rationale

**Figure 1** establishes the experimental design, disease induction timelines, and morphological/histological characterization of the two complementary in vivo murine models of chronic liver injury:

1. **Toxic Liver Fibrosis ($\text{CCl}_4$ Model):**
   * Repeated intraperitoneal injections of carbon tetrachloride ($\text{CCl}_4$) leading to progressive pericentral hepatocyte necrosis, pericentral-to-pericentral bridging fibrosis, and subsequent cirrhosis over 2, 6, and 12 months ($n = 36$ mice total).
   * Mineral oil-treated mice serve as vehicle controls.

2. **Cholestatic Liver Fibrosis (BDL Model):**
   * Common bile duct ligation (BDL) leading to acute/subacute biliary obstruction, high biliary pressure, intense periportal ductular reaction, and periportal-to-periportal fibrosis ($n = 18$ mice total).
   * Comparison groups: Sham controls + vehicle, BDL + vehicle, and BDL + AS0369 (ASBT inhibitor / anti-fibrotic intervention).

## 2. Panel Breakdown & Histological Stains

* **Panel A ($\text{CCl}_4$ Histology & Progression):**
  * **Sirius Red:** Visualizes total fibrillar collagen deposition and fibrous septa formation across Control, 2 months, 6 months, and 12 months $\text{CCl}_4$.
  * **CK19 / GS Double Immunofluorescence:** 
    * **Cytokeratin 19 (CK19, Green):** Marks biliary epithelial cells (cholangiocytes) and proliferating ductular reactive cells.
    * **Glutamine Synthetase (GS, Red):** Marks pericentral hepatocytes to delineate metabolic liver zonation and pericentral injury zones.

* **Panel B (BDL Histology & Therapeutic Reversal):**
  * **Sirius Red:** Shows periportal collagen expansion in BDL mice compared to Sham controls, and therapeutic fibrosis attenuation following AS0369 ASBT inhibition.
  * **CK19 Immunostaining:** Highlights the massive expansion of the intrahepatic biliary tree in BDL and its normalization under ASBTi treatment.

* **Panels C--F (Quantitative Morphometry & Experimental Workflows):**
  * Morphometric quantification of collagen proportionate area (% Sirius Red area).
  * Quantification of CK19-positive ductular reaction area.
  * Multi-omics sampling roadmap (RNA-sequencing and Mass Spectrometry proteomics from matched liver tissue aliquots).

## 4. Nature Communications Figure Legend

> **Figure 1: Histopathological characterization and multi-omics workflow of toxic ($\text{CCl}_4$) and cholestatic (BDL) murine liver fibrosis.**  
> **(A)** Representative liver tissue sections of mineral oil control and $\text{CCl}_4$-treated mice at 2, 6, and 12 months stained with Sirius Red (collagen fibers, top) and double-immunostained for Cytokeratin 19 (CK19, green, cholangiocytes) and Glutamine Synthetase (GS, red, pericentral hepatocytes, bottom).  
> **(B)** Representative liver sections from Sham-operated controls + vehicle, BDL + vehicle, and BDL treated with the ASBT inhibitor AS0369, stained with Sirius Red and CK19.  
> **(C--F)** Quantitative image analysis of fibrotic area, ductular proliferation, and experimental workflow illustrating parallel RNA-sequencing and LC-MS/MS proteomic profiling from matched liver lobes.
